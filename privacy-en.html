Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

Roast Route is a map of coffee bars and cycling cafés. This policy explains which data we store, why, for how long, and what your rights are. We deliberately keep it small: we only ask for what the app really needs.

Who is responsible?

Roast Route is created and run by Milan Meert, Belgium.
Questions about your data? Email meertmilan@gmail.com.

Your location ("Near me") is only used in your own browser to calculate distances. We do not send it anywhere and do not store it.

Importing your own GPX: the file is read in your own browser. It only ends up in our database if you save the route yourself. When you add a coffee bar, we only send the points of that detour to the route planner.

Sharing a route: the points of your route are then included in the link. Anyone who receives the link can see those points. So don't share a link with your home address as the starting point if you don't want to.

In your browser we store your language choice and favourites (localStorage) so the app remembers them. These are not tracking cookies, and we use no advertising or analytics tools.

Who processes the data for us?

  • Supabase: database and accounts. Servers in the EU (Stockholm, Sweden).
  • Brevo (France): sends the confirmation email, password emails and the newsletter.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website. Like any web server, GitHub sees your IP address.
  • OpenStreetMap: provides the map and looks up addresses. The map server sees your IP address.
  • BRouter (brouter.de, Germany) and, as a backup, FOSSGIS (routing.openstreetmap.de, Germany): calculate cycling routes when you use "Plan a route". These servers see the points of your route and your IP address. We do not store your routes.
  • Google Fonts, jsDelivr and unpkg: provide fonts and code libraries. They also see your IP address.

We never sell your data and do not share it with anyone else.

How long do we keep it?

As long as you have an account. If you delete your account, we immediately erase your profile, profile photo, favourites, saved routes, reviews and suggestions. If you unsubscribe from the newsletter, you will no longer receive emails.

Your rights

  • Access and correction: ask by email which data we hold about you, or have it corrected.
  • Erasure: click Delete account in the app. Everything is erased immediately.
  • Withdrawing consent: untick Monthly email in the app, or click the unsubscribe link in the newsletter.
  • Complaint: not satisfied? You can lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (dataprotectionauthority.be).

Security

Passwords are stored encrypted. The database is set up so that everyone can only see and change their own data. The connection to the app always uses HTTPS.
